Scope and method
Three user-requested GPT-6-Luna agents audited disjoint file-order batches
of 866 arXiv-only bibliography entries without DOI fields. Root also checked
27 arXiv entries with missing/nonstandard journal fields (one MathOverflow
entry was excluded). Sources: arxiv-symmetricfunctions get_paper,
paper-cache find/archive metadata, and publisher-deposited Crossref records.
No bulk PDF downloads were needed. This is discovery, not a bibliography patch.

Review cautions
A normalized title/author match is a candidate, not proof that all metadata
are ready to paste. Crossref can give online year instead of issue year;
lane 1 did not retain exact date-parts. Lane 0 prefers print/online year but
may fall back to deposit creation year. Recheck dates before integration.
Preserve original citation keys, authors, mathematical title capitalization,
and arXiv identifiers. Distinguish journal articles from book chapters.
Do not merge duplicate keys without checking all citation sites.
Future assigned issue years require an online-publication check.
